How to do CPRPlace the heel of one hand in the centre of the chest (lower breastbone), other hand on top, interlock fingers. Push down 5–6 cm at 100–120 beats/min. After 30 compressions give 2 rescue breaths (if trained). Continue until help arrives. Untrained? Do hands-only CPR.
Posture aidOpen your phone camera beside the patient so you can see your arms and their chest. Shoulders directly over hands, arms straight. The metronome keeps the rhythm.
CPR is for adults For children use one hand, for infants two fingers — be gentler. This assistant guides adult CPR. Take a certified First Aid course when you can.

Camera Aid

Frame a wound or burn to capture it for your incident report. The crosshair helps keep the camera steady and centred. Captured images stay on your device unless you choose AI analysis, which sends the image to FirstMinute's AI vision service for processing.
How to frame a wound1. If safe, gently remove clothing around the wound. 2. Hold the camera ~30 cm away, directly above. 3. Fill the white frame with the wound and a small margin of healthy skin. 4. Keep still; good light helps.
This is not a diagnosisThe image is captured for your own record and to share with a hospital. It does not tell you the type or severity of the injury. A trained clinician must assess it.
